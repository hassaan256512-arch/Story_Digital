About the page

Stories that fit in one sitting.

Story Digital started as a simple idea: not everyone has time to sit down with a novel, but almost everyone has a minute to spare while scrolling. So the stories here are written to be read start to finish in that minute, without losing the shape of a real story — a beginning, a turn, and an ending worth reading twice.

Every post is written from scratch, edited by hand, and shared for free. This website exists as a home base for the page — a place to see what it's about, browse by genre, and get pointed back to the latest story on Instagram.
