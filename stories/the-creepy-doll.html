The Creepy Doll

Short story · about 4 min read

No one in the antique shop wants the old porcelain doll. Then one customer decides to take it home.

Sunlight filtered through the windows into the antique shop, creeping across the floor towards an object thought to have been forgotten, a doll. The porcelain skin was and its eyes carried an emptiness the doll was experiencing. The shopkeeper had tried offering the doll to many but always ended up getting rejected, consequently, the doll remained in his shop, until one day.

A customer entered the shop, the shopkeeper looked at him and offered a smile, a gesture he was used to. The customer seemed to be in a conundrum while choosing through the various objects, after looking through all of them, he picked a certain object which shocked the shopkeeper, it was the doll itself.

The shopkeeper couldn’t believe his eyes, someone was really buying the doll, the one object many others rejected because of its old and rusty look. However, this customer didn’t seem like them, not at all. The shopkeeper’s curiosity was piqued as he saw the customer approaching and asking to buy the doll.

After purchasing, the shopkeeper told the customer the truth to which the customer seemed equally shocked. The shopkeeper explained how no one wanted to buy the doll, “You know sir, no one bought this doll so far except you, it has been sitting here in this very shop since the day it arrived, people say they don’t want to buy this because it is old and secondly, it gives off creepy vibes”.

Upon hearing this, the customer’s eyes widened but he didn’t think much of it, responding, “Oh, well, that’s odd isn’t it? I found this doll rather interesting to add to my collection at home.” After the conversation, the man came back home and placed the doll on the couch while he himself went to the kitchen.

The doll’s eyes, slowly but surely, started moving and followed where the man went, unbeknownst to him, he had not bought a doll, but something far more sinister, waiting to pounce on its prey, which of course was going to be the man himself. As the man returned from the kitchen, he stopped dead in his tracks as he noticed the doll had moved slightly from its place.

The man approached the doll and sat beside it, trying to notice something which he couldn’t quite put his finger on. Thinking that he was imagining things since he was tired, he went to his bedroom to catch his well deserved rest. As he lay in bed, he heard footsteps that he knew weren’t his, they were faint, almost silent but unmistakably there.

His eyes widened and he quickly sat up in his bed, trying to hear the footsteps better but to his horror, they stopped.

He got out and quickly opened the door of his room, only to find the hallway eerily dark and quiet. He made his way to the living room and found another shocking sight, the doll wasn’t on the couch anymore where he had left it before.

He frantically started searching, trying to find where the doll went. Half an hour had passed since he started searching and still found no sign of the doll. There was one room he hadn’t checked, it was the storage room. Just then, a zephyr brushed against his skin, causing him to shiver and jolt up, he heard a faint, soft whisper in his ear, “Find me.”

The man quickly darted towards the storage room and as soon as he opened the door, the chilly air hit his body. He saw the sight which made his stomach churn, the doll was right in front of him, facing him with those creepy, distant eyes. Just then, he heard another whisper which seemed to come from all directions, “You will soon discover your fate...”

The man’s eyes widened in horror and as soon as he tried to run, the doors shut behind him with a powerful thud as if someone violently slammed the doors shut. Turning around, the man saw the doll slowly rising from its spot and was now floating in the air. The man’s breath hitched, body turning pale due to the sheer amount of fear coursing through his veins as the doll’s body started shifting. Its limbs elongating and twisting, its face embracing a wicked, evil grin. “You’re not just discovering your fate, but becoming it.” The whisper sent a strong shiver down the spine of the man, causing his body to straighten fully.

The next day, the house was silent and back to normal, the doll was on the couch, as if nothing had happened prior. However, one thing did happen, the man, was now just a relic of the past...

The End
