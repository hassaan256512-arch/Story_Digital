Fallen Angels

8 chapters · about 57 min read

A Los Angeles journalist follows the news as an asteroid heads for Earth, and what lands is not what anyone expected.

Chapter 1

The season of crisp spring in the city of Los Angeles, California, had just started. Ethan woke up to the noise of cars honking and usual routine being carried out in the city. He quickly jumped out of bed, heading straight for the closet from where he picked up his clothes and got dressed. The sound of birds chirping echoed through his house as he was the only one living there.

He glanced out of the window to see the cityscape illuminated by soft glowing sunlight shining from above as he sipped coffee. His phone popped up with a message from his colleague, “Ethan, coming to work?” the simple question made him almost spill his coffee as he quickly looked at the time “10:00 A.M.” the clock read, almost making his heart jump into his throat as he had never been an hour late.

“Im arriving, just woke up a bit late.” He typed vigorously, fingers flying over the keyboard as he chugged down the coffee and hurried to his car, firing up the engine, the car growled and rolled out of the parking and onto the street as Ethan headed to his office. After a brief 10 minute drive, he arrived at the media house where he worked as a journalist. The cool air emanating from the A.C hit Ethan like a wave as he entered the familiar building and walked straight towards his room, during the way, he greeted many fellow journalist and other staff members who worked at the media house.

The door swung open and Ethan was finally in the room he was assigned since he had started this job and he absolutely loved it despite negative views from his family. His colleague, James, turned around in his seat to look at Ethan with an amused expression, “You do know that you’re an hour late, do you?” he asked, teasing Ethan as he always did. “Yeah, yeah I am an hour late and I know damn well that you’ll be roasting me for the rest of my shift here.”

“Precisely, you’re learning so quickly, Ethan.” His colleague laughed, lightly punching Ethan’s shoulder as Ethan sat in his chair, stretching his legs, “Anyways, what’s the news currently?” Ethan inquired and James scanned the screen of the computer in front of him before answering Ethan’s question. “Nothing much, man, just recently NASA issued an alert that an asteroid will pass close by but it also has a chance of impacting the planet.”

Ethan shrugged, “Eh, it happens every other day, what else?” James seemed serious as he glanced at Ethan’s dismissive gesture in a shocked manner, “Dude, its not usual, the asteroid is of a big size and its impact point is difficult to determine.” James added matter-of-factly, causing Ethan to stop his dismissive gesture, “Well, they say that about every other asteroid, don’t they?” James shrugged, “Well, they do, just see the news bro.” He tilted the computer’s screen to show Ethan the news. “NASA has just released a fresh alert for the entire world as an unusual asteroid is headed towards Earth. Chilling revelation is that NASA is facing difficulty to properly calculate the impact zone of the asteroid if it impacts with experts hoping it misses earth and goes away. Reportedly, the asteroid was seen performing vague left and right manoeuvres which asteroids don’t normally do since they have no thrusters.”

Ethan swallowed air nervously, “What? This can’t be true, how is an asteroid able to perform such manoeuvres?” Ethan was left with more questions than answers. “Well, I guess you should get reporting as well.” James added, unsure himself of what was coming humanity’s way.

Ethan sat straight in his chair, fixing his posture before recording his news broadcast, “Good morning, today’s bulletin includes a warning from NASA about an asteroid on its way to our planet. According to various reports, NASA is facing difficulty in tracking and calculating where the asteroid may impact. Consequently, it has warned the entire planet to stay conscious and other space agencies should also be tracking the asteroid. Difficulty in determining collision point stems from the fact that the asteroid is able to perform slight manoeuvers in its path, swinging left and right while maintaining a steady speed. Important thing to know is that asteroids don’t have thrusters and are just chunks of rock, meaning they can’t manoeuver in space or roam around, that’s all for this bulletin, see you soon.”

“Man, conflicting reports are emerging from NASA. Some say the asteroid will miss Earth, some say it will impact and cause massive destruction.” James read through the article published by a news outlet. “So, even NASA is not sure about what is coming our way.” Ethan added, rubbing his forehead. “Well, don’t worry, it will be alright.” He tried to calm himself and his colleague despite having intrusive thoughts. Pulling out his phone, he scrolled through his social media app and saw thousands of posts already circulating on the internet about the asteroid approaching Earth—it was now a widely known news being broadcasted on almost every news channel.

Suddenly, the computer screen in front of James and Ethan flickered, showing an executive of NASA giving a press conference to the media outlets which included their media cameraman as well. “Good morning to everyone, today, I stand here to inform you all on a very important alert. To brief about it, our systems detected an asteroid headed towards Earth on a collision course. While a chance of it missing our planet exists, it is being calculated to be low, which raises alarms for us. However, my advise to all is to stay calm and follow any guidelines which law enforcement or the government provides, thank you.”

As soon as Ethan and James listened to the press briefing by the NASA executive, the feeling of unease grew stronger in their chests, gripping their hearts tightly. “I have never seen NASA so nervous about their discoveries before.” James confessed, getting a bit scared. “I just hope it misses our planet and everything goes back to normal.” He added, sitting straighter in his chair. In the meantime, Ethan dialled up his family and contacted them, “Hi, mom, have you seen the news?”—Back at home, her mother picked up the phone, tensing slightly at Ethan’s worried tone, “No sweetie, what happened? Is everything alright?” Ethan rubbed his head, thinking the response to her mother’s obliviousness. “Well mom, NASA has just informed that they have been monitoring an asteroid that is on a collision course with Earth and the frightening thing is that NASA can’t calculate its striking point meaning it can impact literally anywhere, so I just wanted to tell you to stay cautious and don’t panic.”

At the horrifying news, Ethan’s mother had her breath caught in her throat, “Really? Oh god, I feared for a day like this.” Ethan immediately realized that his mother was having an anxiety attack and attempted to soothe her through the phone. “Mom, relax, its going to be okay, there are still chances of it missing our planet and I hope it misses.” Ethan intentionally hid the part that the chances of the asteroid missing are very slim. However, this helped calm his mother down a bit from her anxiety and Ethan felt relieved. “Take care of yourself too, sweetheart.” His mother said at last.

Chapter 2

Just as the call ended, Ethan glanced at the screen of his computer and saw a countdown happening in the news channels. “What? A countdown?” the ticking clock had 24 hours on it. “So the asteroid will crash in 24 hours?” his colleague nodded, “Yeah, the countdown is from NASA.” The words hung heavy in the air, both men falling silent as the only sound in the room was of the soft humming of the air conditioner. The tension was palpable. “Tell me you’re kidding, right?” Ethan asked, turning to face his colleague James who looked quite nervous.

“Ethan, relax, its not like its the end of the world, just an asteroid. Also, if it crashes into the ocean, we will all be fine.” He attempted at easing some tension but Ethan was the over thinker. “Don’t you realize that the asteroid hitting the ocean is just a ‘chance’ and it can impact on the ground too, causing panic and perhaps, casualties.” James shook his head, taking a deep breath to steel himself.

“Dude, take a deep breath and relax, for God’s sake. You’re driving yourself and me insane by your over thinking.” Ethan felt a bit offended, “Oh? You think I am driving us both insane? Even NASA doesn’t know what that asteroid is, or worse, if it even is an asteroid and you’re here sitting relaxed without preparing for the worst case scenario.”

James knew that Ethan was just tense and hyperventilating so he kept his cool and attempted to cool down Ethan. “Who said I am ignorant, I am calm and composed, just like you should be dude. Over thinking and anger won’t solve an asteroid hitting us.” Ethan buried his head in his hands, exhaling audibly, trying to soothe his frayed nerves.

In a sudden, unexpected moment, the door swung open revealing Chloe who was also a journalist. Her expression giving away that she was exhausted and spent. “Guys, have you seen the countdown for that damn asteroid? Its been freaking me out since the past I don’t know how much time.” She huffed, sitting on the couch in the studio room. Ethan nodded, “Yes, I have seen it and I don’t know why NASA suddenly got so good at building suspense.”

“They are not even providing any further information regarding that asteroid, just a stupid countdown.” Chloe muttered annoyingly, glancing nervously at the countdown for a beat too long. The room was once again enveloped in silence which was broken by James asking a question, “So, are we just going to sit and wait for that countdown to hit zero or what?” Ethan turned his head towards James to answer his question. “Of course we won’t be sitting here for a whole day, I guess just visit your relatives, friends and family and try to enjoy as much as you can.” Upon hearing Ethan’s words, Chloe glanced at him with a slight amused smile, “You really think the world is ending, Ethan? That’s dramatic as hell.”

Ethan was taken aback by her words as well, the same words which James said to him a few moments ago. “I mean we don’t know for sure. I am saying just in case we should visit our loved ones.” Ethan defended himself. “I don’t mean the end of the world, I am just saying to go to our loved ones and enjoy.” Chloe’s expression softened as she nodded, “Don’t worry Ethan, I was just joking, I know you just want us to meet our loved ones but don’t be afraid, its not the end of the world.”

“Yes exactly, that’s what I said to Ethan.” James chimed in, agreeing with Chloe. “Okay, okay, I lose, you both win.” Said Ethan, holding his hands up in a playful gesture.

After their shift was over, Ethan and his colleagues packed their bags and went to their respective homes. While driving, Ethan kept recalling the countdown so much that he had opened up the news on his phone which was on his car’s dashboard and he drove while listening to the updates and also noting the time on the clock.

As he got home, he took a quick shower and then turned on his T.V to watch news. “This just in, NASA has confirmed that only approximately twelve hours remain until the impact of the asteroid. Though NASA admitted in having difficulty tracking and identifying its exact strike point, they have managed to calculate a rough estimate area on the planet where it could impact.” Ethan sat up straighter, completely intrigued by the new knowledge as he desperately wanted to know where the asteroid may impact. The reason why it was so special was because this asteroid was unlike any other, manoeuvring by itself and travelling at insane speed. “First possible impact location is said to be the United States Of America, most likely near California and Nevada. Second location can be near the Indian ocean while the third could be again in the United States near Texas, Colorado, Utah, Wyoming or Arizona.”

Ethan’s breath caught in his throat upon listening to the coordinates which provided a boost to his already spiking depression and anxiety. “Darn it. Why near where I live, why only us?” but then he remembered an impact point near Indian ocean and hoped for the asteroid to crash into the ocean and not cause any havoc upon humanity.

Back at NASA, astronomers and astrophysicists kept studying the pattern of the asteroid which was behaving very unusually. “Look, its changing position again.”—“It has been doing that since the past few hours.” The monitoring room fell into deafening silence as everyone held their breath.

Chapter 3

In space, satellites struggled to keep track of the asteroid which seemed to have accelerated a little compared to before when it was first seen. It was now super close to Earth and could impact anytime, anywhere. Astronomers at NASA watched with bated breath as the asteroid started to slowly penetrate the Earth’s atmosphere, making its official entry into the planet’s perimeter. The asteroid’s surface started burning and caught fire, heading straight towards the ground in flames. The air around the invading rock seemed to twist and turn in ways science couldn’t explain. The asteroid kept accelerating further as Earth’s gravity pulled it with more force by each passing second. The rocks on ground started trembling as the asteroid approached the impact zone and moments later, a BOOM.

Everything went silent at first but the silence didn’t stay for long as a shockwave swept everything out of its way in a 5 kilometre radius, followed by a small earthquake which was felt in nearby villages and small towns. Ethan who had sacrificed his sleep to witness the asteroid impact couldn’t see it and frowned, but suddenly, the news headline shifted and the anchor read a report he just received. “Big breaking! Residents of the towns located in southern California and Nevada reported feeling slight tremors and strong winds. The towns which were affected are Nipton, Baker and Primm. Residents further reported that they felt aftershocks followed by a slight ‘shockwave’. Our reporters are currently gathering further information on whether it was an earthquake or something else.”

Ethan practically jumped out of his couch, his eyes went wide as he quickly switched to the channel showing the countdown to that asteroid’s crash. To his immense horror, the countdown was at zero. He immediately switched back to the news channel where he had seen the reports of the towns of Baker, Primm and Nipton feeling the ground shaking with aftershocks and what felt like a shockwave. Ethan’s heart pounded in his chest wildly, his mind flooded to the brim with intrusive thoughts as he knew that a natural earthquake never causes a shockwave. He immediately did some research on the Internet and found out that the very symptoms told in the news were of those of an asteroid crashing on the surface of the earth.

He immediately phoned James, his colleague, “Hey James, have you seen the news?”. The reply came quick. “Yes Ethan, I think the asteroid might have crashed in Nevada or Southern California. The news is covering it up by saying its a natural earthquake.”

“Its all cheap propaganda, the news is hiding it from us. Its an asteroid! Listen, we must raise our voice against this propaganda and demand the truth from the media.” Ethan’s tone was firm and determined as he believed everyone had the right to know the truth. He then posted a video on his social media account and James also posted, telling people about the asteroid impact that the media was hiding from people. Within minutes, thousands of people viewed the posts and began demanding the truth on what exactly happened because all had a same question, a natural earthquake never causes a shockwave. Ethan was happy when he saw thousands of people supporting his cause to know the truth but Ethan had one question. If the asteroid crashed, which it did, then why were the media hiding it?

Soon as hours flew by, media channels began broadcasting the truth, thanks to the pressure Ethan had built since he was a journalist himself. “Big breaking, NASA has confirmed that an asteroid has just crashed in Southern California with the impact being felt in mid California and western parts of Nevada. Eyewitnesses saw they felt the ground tremble beneath their feet and thought it was an earthquake but then felt a shockwave as a gust of strong wind suddenly appeared out of the blue. Another horrifying detail emerging is electrical devices and appliances are found to be randomly malfunctioning or hanging for no apparent reasons, from light bulbs to mobiles to PCs, everything which has electric connection is beginning to malfunction with fluctuations with experts saying that something might be messing with the electric field of these devices which is not a normal issue.”

Ethan felt dread building up in his chest as he listened to every single word. Not to mention that he was also a conspiracy theorist who loved to hear and make theories regarding certain topics. He then received a call from his mom who spoke urgently, her voice almost trembling. “Ethan, have you felt any tremors?” the words crashed against Ethan’s ears when he heard his mother’s tense tone. “No mom, I didn’t feel any tremors here in L.A, but the news has been keeping me updated lately on what is happening and I have tried connecting the dots which are leading to a horrifying reality.” Her mother frowned in curiosity, scared of what Ethan might tell her. “What is it, sweetheart?” she inquired, eager as much as frightened to know the truth. “I think its an asteroid impact, mom, and at first it sounded like a conspiracy but a natural earthquake never produces a shockwave and now the media has admitted it as well.” His mom gasped, her eyes widening in terror as she struggled to grasp the harsh truth. “That asteroid? The one which NASA was talking about?”--“Yes mom, the exact asteroid, although the exact impact site has not been disclosed yet, just stay safe and keep yourself updated with any government announcements and I will keep you updated as well.” Ethan reassured before the call came to an abrupt end, the only sound audible was of the static beep echoing through the channel. “Mom? Hello?” Ethan spoke, pulling his phone away from his ear to glance at the screen and he saw his phone glitching, acting like it had a mind of its own.

“ugh, come on.” Ethan tried gaining back the control of his phone but the phone refused to listen. He set it aside on the table before going to the window of his apartment building to see if anything was going on. However, to his surprise, the city remained normal—almost too normal, the perfect feeling of the calm before the storm.

A team of geologists arrived at the site of the impact where the asteroid was half submerged into the ground inside a big crater. Shockingly, their van broke down while they were not even close to it but could see it from afar with their eyes. “Mike, what happened?” one geologist asked the driver who looked just as confused. “I don’t know, man, this thing won’t move any further, guess we gotta hike up to it.” The driver responded with a chuckle as one man got off and suddenly vanished without a trace, leaving behind a soft gust of wind as if something had just passed by with insane speed. “What the hell? Where’s Jay!?” Sean panicked, “HE WAS JUST RIGHT HERE! I heard something akin to a scream.” Sean glanced around with wide eyes, noticing that the others were also just as breathless when suddenly they heard a weird mechanical sound which seemed to be vibrating through the very air they were breathing and they managed to catch a glimpse of something they were never meant to see.

The car lay abandoned at the side of the road with all of the geologists mysteriously vanishing without a trace. Their communication equipment buzzing without them responding.

In the towns of Baker, Primm and Nipton, residents felt their devices malfunction and act unusually, some died randomly, others simply didn’t function as instructed. Some people even had their cars randomly breaking down or stopping for no apparent reason. Sophia who lived in Primm glanced out of the window of her house, taking the scene of her neighbourhood street as she sensed something which her eyes couldn’t see yet but the hair on the nape of her neck stood up, her heart slowly beginning to beat faster and faster. She clutched her chest, unsure of what was happening to her, her mind growing fuzzy and dizzy when there she saw it-on the road-a tall, humanoid yet non-human entity, standing taller than an average human and its features hidden by its dark colour. Just as she spotted the otherworldly creature, she passed out.

Similar eerie situations began occurring with more and more people living in the towns located near the asteroid impact side. However, authorities seemed to be late in their response to launch an official investigation. John, an old enough man who lived in the town of Baker saw his wife collapse out of nowhere “Maya!” he blurted out, rushing to assist his elder wife but she didn’t open her eyes, momentarily passed out, her skin turning pale. “Oh god, oh no!” John tried dialling the helpline number but his phone only spoke static. In panic, he put the phone aside and shook his wife attempting to have her conscious. Just as he was about to get water, he heard a soft hissing sound coming from everywhere and nowhere at once, confusing John as he hurriedly made his way into the kitchen but there he met something he had never expected. A black figure which slowly extended its hand towards him before his vision blurred into total darkness.

The lights of every town near the asteroid impact zone flickered before dying, plunging everything into darkness. No one knew what was happening, why it was happening and what exactly was the cure to it-or was there even one? At the police headquarters, officers and senior inspectors along with sergeants were puzzled by the reports which kept flowing in through the helpline number. Some people reporting seeing figures, others reporting mysterious disappearances while some reported their electrical devices malfunctioning. The police chief sat in his seat in a conundrum. On one hand, people were demanding an investigation, the truth behind what was happening. On the other, he wasn’t sure how to investigate the matter because it was happening so fast and so massively that it was extremely difficult to keep track of. Just then, a lieutenant walked in with a rather big filein his arm. His face giving away that he was in a panic. “Chief, You have to see this. The records that we have managed to gather so far, my head is spinning from keeping track of all of them and there’s more coming in.” He put the file onto the chief’s desk and with a heavy sigh, the chief opened it and started scrolling through the pages one by one, his stress worsening with each page turned. “The public demands a press conference or some sort of announcement which will help clarify things. Mayors have also started taking the cases into notice and are questioning the effectiveness of law enforcement units under our command against.. Whatever is happening.” The Lieutenant briefed the chief who was rubbing his forehead in frustration. “This seems to be a bigger trouble than we think.” The chief began, exhaling as if letting out his stress, “Call in the media, I’ll hold a press conference with other lieutenants and sergeants to address the public.” The decision was made and the lieutenant immediately got to work.

Back in Ethan’s home, he managed to gain control of his mobile phone after restarting it and opened his social media app just to check about any updates regarding the current situation from his OSINT network. What he found on it, sent chills down his spine. As he scrolled through the reels and posts, his heart seemed to sink deeper into the abyss of his own body. Videos showed people collapsed on the floor with their skin pale and a low resolution video appeared to capture a tall black figure standing near a street light pole before the footage glitched. He read through the posts and comments of people which only terrified him further. “THEY-ARE-HERE-”, “SOMETHING-IS-WRONG”, “THEY-ARE-NOT-HUMANS.” Ethan’s chest began tightening in horror after reading the cryptic messages and seeing the brutal videos and posts which sent unpleasant shivers down his spine. Just then, he wanted to see what the actual news was telling and turned on his TV, the screen had lost its colors surprisingly and was only displaying channels in black and white color. An emergency press conference appeared on all news channels where the police chief along with lieutenants and sergeants was standing at a podium, delivering his speech to the public. “Greetings to all residents of California and Nevada. Today I stand here at the podium of the state to address a very serious matter which involves our safety and lives. We have been encountering some really strange reports lately from the public and its really unfortunate to inform that our personnel have been facing difficulties to properly investigate into the matter. As a result, we’ve decided to deploy national guard troops across California and Nevada to ensure safety of civilians and sites. Odd enough, we have also received reports from people which say they sighted tall black figures which appear to be humanoid before they disappear with lightning fast speed, although investigation hasn’t proven it, we can’t say anything as a confirmed report. At last, stay at home, lock windows and may god bless us all.” Just then, the TV screen flickered again with static and for a split moment Ethan could have sworn that he saw something strange, it appeared to be a tall figure standing behind the chief, something which wasn’t supposed to be seen.

“Not great not terrible.” Ethan huffed in frustration, already fed up by dealing with faulty electronics and communication lines but now also having to deal with a lockdown. “God, why only us?” Ethan asked, the question hanging heavy in the air as the temperature slowly dropped by itself, sending cool shivers across his skin. He went to the window of his apartment and glanced out, seeing the streets mostly empty which was greatly surprising for a bustling city such as Los Angeles. He caught the sight of some national guard troops being deployed from a truck, their expressions seeming like they themselves were questioning why they were there in the first place. In a sudden turn of events, Ethan’s ears picked up a soft yet persistent hissing sound which felt like it came from everywhere and nowhere at once. He immediately began searching his entire apartment, inch by inch, trying to find the source of the hissing noise, his senses put on full alert. Despite searching relentlessly, he found absolutely nothing, leaving him confused and clueless of what the hissing sound exactly was and why it was there. He tried hearing it better and it felt like gas leaking out of a pipe but he had checked all pipes. While rubbing his head and trying to figure out what the hissing sound was, his TV suddenly flickered back to life, catching his attention in a millisecond.

Chapter 4

On the TV, a news broadcast was at play where the anchor reported a civilian airliner crash due to a technical fault, unfortunately no one survived that flight. Ethan rubbed his temple in grief and sorrow but the next detail made his bones cold. “The plane reportedly flew over the zone where the asteroid had crashed.” In an instant, Ethan picked up his phone and scrolled through social media posts where people were talking about the recent plane crash. He posted the update that the aircraft flew over the asteroid impact zone and people became horrified. Ethan dialled up James’ number and he thankfully picked up in time. “James, listen.” Ethan spoke, his voice nervous, “Whatever has crashed on our planet, its not just another asteroid. Its something which I fear that we can’t comprehend yet but why isn’t the government taking it seriously?” His voice rose a bit in anger and a mix of panic. James on the other hand remained calm and composed. “Relax Ethan, the government is working on it.” Ethan snorted, clearly not interested in James’s calm response, “Oh yeah? And who told you that? You got connections in the government?” Ethan retorted sarcastically. “No man, just relax, everything will be okay-” suddenly the call started glitching, the audio twisting and bending in ways a human brain couldn’t process. Ethan’s eyes blew wide as he immediately pulled the phone away from his ear to the front of his eyes to see what was going on. The call logs were glitching as if his phone was getting hacked.

Blissfully unaware of the outside world, Ethan was left in a tricky puzzle, whether to get out of Los Angeles and head to his parents house but he knew the situation doesn’t allow so, or stay in his apartment in Los Angeles and risk the survival alone with almost no contact with anyone due to a faulty phone and communication line. The unknown phenomenon which was occurring with people in California and Nevada now began to spread, faster than expected. Before anyone knew it, it had already reached Arizona, Texas and Kansas. The same reports which emerged from California days ago were now emerging from the other infested states with police and national guard facing difficulty in keeping up with the security and taking casualties with most of the men going missing in action. People on social media who still had access began putting pressure on the state, demanding an answer to whatever was happening with majority of the population in severe panic. Conspiracy theorists didn’t help either as they fueled the chaos and distress of people by claiming it was an alien invasion or worse, the government itself might be involved. One person online posted a comment with a screenshot saying that his phone randomly typed some ancient looking text which doesn’t exist on any keyboard in the world. Soon, mayors of the infested states began to take notice and took the matter to the President who had to chair an emergency meeting with the heads of the army and airforce along with the secretary of defence. “Gentlemen, we have gathered here despite the rough situation in our country. What is going on?” the President asked, his voice firm and demanding answers without delay. “Sir, reports have been emerging from almost all states about spotting unknown humanoid figures which glitch around, people mysteriously vanishing never to be seen again, electronics losing control and recently one civilian airliner lost all speed, stalled and crashed, unfortunately ending everyone’s life on board.” The secretary of defence answered back, rummaging through the files filled with cases. The room fell silent once again, filled with the low hum of the air conditioners before the President spoke again, “And do we have insights on what we are facing possibly? Aliens?” his tone now growing serious and slightly worried. “I am afraid not, sir.” The secretary’s answer caused the tension in the room to rise to extreme levels, each officer and official held their breath for a moment before exhaling. The room grew colder as everyone exchanged glances at each other, hoping for someone or something to break the silence. “What do you mean by that?” The President inquired, clearly not happy after knowing that they had no knowledge about the threats. “You’re telling me that our country is dying from within and we don’t know what is actually happening?” his voice rising a bit in frustration. “What is our status right now?” The President shifted his attention to the secretary of state who stuttered initially upon being suddenly in the spotlight. “Yes, right sir, we have deployed national guard troops and advised people to stay in home and avoid going outdoors. That’s the best we can do right now without much knowledge of the threats we are facing.” The President nodded, burying half of his face in his hands, “We need to evaluate, quick. What will it take to gain further knowledge about that asteroid and potentially, whatever got off of it.” The question hung heavy in the air as the military and air force generals exchanged nervous glances, hoping their branches dont have to carry out the extremely dangerous reconnaissance missions. “We would have to send a search party of geologists and scientists to inspect the impact site and collect the materials from there to be delivered to our research facilities.” Secretary of the state answered but the Secretary of defence fired back, “We had already sent one team and we lost contact, none of the men have made it back. This is a way serious matter than we perceive and I fear we can’t even approach the site.” The President seemed alarmed, the burden of the entire nation increasing on his shoulders. “I need you to assemble a team of experts and arrange all necessary equipment for them to communicate with HQ and also have alternative routes of comms ready. We need them to get as much information as possible of what has entered our planet.” The President ordered swiftly, despite knowing that an earlier team never came back. The secretaries and generals nodded before getting to work and the came to an end. The President stood alone before a large window, glancing out at the city, taking a deep breath because he knew that the entire country’s future and perhaps survival was dependent on him.

The strange phenomenon had now slowly started getting violent, attacking people and law enforcement units alike with no way yet of stopping the attacks from happening. Ethan sat alone in his apartment, disturbed by the events transpiring before him. He absolutely hated being cut off from the daily news which he was connected to as a journalist. The devices kept glitching occasionally, meaning he had no reliable connection with anyone. He let out an exhale of exhaustion, tired of being trapped in a lockdown alone where the walls seemed to close in on him. Curiosity got the better of him and he walked towards the door before unlocking it with a click which echoed through his entire apartment due to the utter silence otherwise. Outside, the scene was pure apocalyptic. Dead streets, no such sign of people and clouds covering the sun, causing a white cloudy weather. Ethan was somehow unfazed by the eerie environment as he veered into potentially lethal territory by venturing out in the open despite being told to stay home and lock all doors and windows. He slowly crept up to a small checkpoint formed by the law enforcement national guards deployed in the city. Upon spotting Ethan, the guards raised their guns “Sir, put your hands up and state your intentions!” They demanded in a commanding tone, causing Ethan to be speechless for a moment before clarifying that he is a civilian and means no harm. The guards immediately instructed him to go back inside and take cover but Ethan refused, causing the guards to speak more urgently, trying to explain how critical the situation was as the strange phenomenon could occur any moment, potentially causing something unexpected. “Listen, I just wanted to get to know what was happening outside-” Before he could finish his argument, a strange low frequency sound echoed through the empty streets, vibrating the very air around them. The guards froze in preparedness, guns steady and aimed, eyes scanning the surroundings swiftly, trying to locate the threat which had just made a vocal presence signal. Ethan immediately hurried to the nearest hiding spot when suddenly he heard gunshots and whipped his head to look at the guards shooting at something. As he hopped behind a small bench, peeking through its covers, he saw the guards trying to neutralise what seemed like a tall, black humanoid entity nearly one and a half times the size of an average human, its elongated limbs further accentuating its eerie appearance and its face where the eyes and mouth were supposed to be, was just a face without features to be recognised. It swiftly charged towards the guards, seemingly unfazed by the bullets as one guard was suspended in the air, held by the neck from the Entity’s two-mouth claw like hands. The other guard shot at the entity’s body but it didn’t have any effect and he soon also fell. Ethan was carefully holding his breath, trying his utmost best not to make any sounds which may give his position away. His mind occupied with thoughts on how to escape this lethal situation where death would be guaranteed if the creature spotted his sorry soul.

Chapter 5

Back at the impact site, the team of expert geologists and archaeologists arrived with a special military convoy escorting them, ensuring safety.
