Tether to Reality

15 chapters · about 83 min read

By Hassaan Ijaz

A diner waitress from a modest family finds an unexpected love, but strange things in her new home refuse to stay quiet.

Chapter 1

Lana belonged to a modest and simple family from a town located on the outskirts of the cityscape. Her father worked as a shepherd while she kept tables a Diner to make a living. Financial burdens had made her desperate to do anything for a source of income to support the expenses. At the same Diner, she used to serve a man named Adrian who came from a middle-class background and worked as an engineer in the city. After his shifts, he would occasionally pass by the Diner and have a meal, served by none other than Lana who was the only one present there with another co-worker.

The door swung open and Lana stepped in, greeted by the sight of her mother baking fresh cookies. “Sweetie, you’re back, how was work?” asked her mother while baking, “It was alright, mom, where is dad?” Lana replied, curious to know about her father. Her mother brought the cookies to the table, the smell reaching Lana like a wildfire. “He is late, as usual.” A moment of silence passed, “Lana? Are you okay?” Her mother inquired in concern.

Lana snapped out of her thoughts, “Yeah mom, I am fine.”— “Sweetie, you can tell me if anything is troubling you, no need to hide it from your mother.” Lana reassured that she was okay and they both ate cookies, enjoying the small moment of peace earned through hardships. Unfortunately, The quiet didn’t last long as a knock on the door caused Lana to go and check. She opened the door to see her neighbour standing there, expression grim.

“I regret to inform you but, your father got into an accident and we took him to the hospital but he couldn’t make it.” The plate shattered into pieces, scattering cookies on the floor, their smell turning metallic upon falling from Lana’s trembling hands, her eyes widening impossibly in horror as her breath caught up in her throat. “w-what? What do you mean? What do you MEAN?” her voice grew slowly but surely. Just then her mother came from behind, “What is all the commotion?”

However, her reaction was the same as Lana’s once she also got the news. The next three days were spent in grief and sorrow as friends and relatives came to offer condolences and left after some time. Lana used to cry with her mother all night long since she deeply loved her father and this caused depression to invade her vulnerable mind. Even at burial rituals, Lana would sit with a monotone expression, staring blankly at her mother who was busy in properly burying her husband with her face wet in tears.Upon returning to work a few days later, she was called into the manager’s office where the grumpy manager scolded Lana for not informing about her leave regardless of the emergency without understanding her stress, “Miss Lana, this is your last chance at this job, else you will be fired, now go!.” Lana tried her level best to hold back her tears, a lump forming in her throat as she continued her job—she couldn’t leave too as she was not qualified enough and there were no other diners she could work at.

Continuing to serve the customers, her gaze landed on a familiar face—it was him—Adrian, sitting on a seat near the window. Lana approached him with a book and pen ready in her hand, “Excuse me sir, what would you like to have today,” she asked in her usual polite tone though it cracked a little which Adrian noticed but didn’t probe further. At the end of his meal, Adrian signalled and Lana brought the bill book, placing it elegantly as Adrian put the cash inside with a small piece of paper before handing it back to Lana and heading outside. As Lana brought the bill book back to the counter, she noticed the piece of paper in the cash pile and unfolded it to reveal a number—a phone number.

Chapter 2

Next day came. Same routine followed, though this time, Lana was curious. While serving, she had to find a way to initiate the conversation without sounding weird. “Excuse me sir.” She began softly, “I noticed you left a...phone number along with the cash payment.” Adrian had a knowing look on his face as he nodded, “Indeed I did, thought you’d contact me.”

Lana’s breath hitched at the statement, her mind struggling to grasp Adrian’s intentions, “I... like you.” Adrian confessed as if it was the most normal thing on a random Tuesday. Lana was completely silent for a few moments, she had never expected someone to be so forward with her, so confident with her. She simply stared in shock as Adrian didn’t quite seem like those men who simply flirt, Adrian was somehow different—somehow he had a different aura which Lana could sense.

“E-excuse me sir?” Lana finally spoke in a shaky voice, “You heard me.” Adrian said, taking a slow sip from his glass. “I have seen the way you work, the way you carry yourself and I must say... I am quite, impressed.” Lana took a shallow breath, “But, but I am just a waitress and... and are you serious about this?” she was part excited—part curious at the prospect that someone was finally showing interest in her.

“Yes I believe I do sound quite confident in what I say.” Adrian replied in his cool tone which caused a flutter in Lana’s heart, one she couldn’t ignore. “I- I am not really sure about this, I mean I have a job to look after and-.” Her protests were cut short with Adrian’s voice, “You don’t have to worry about this job, nor worry about money, I can handle it.” Lana couldn’t put it into words but she felt an unexplainable pull towards Adrian. Something inside her was telling her that he may be the one. “Thanks, really, but I don’t want to be a burden on anyone.”—“No need to thank me.” A brief pause, “Want to meet tomorrow at the community park?” Lana was taken aback by the sudden suggestion from the stranger, ‘a date? He really is inviting me to a date?’ her mind screamed that question but she agreed, “Sure. I guess.” Adrian smiled knowingly, “Great, see you tomorrow.” He finished his drink, slipped the cash in the bill book and left, leaving Lana and her thoughts alone.

That day after arriving home, Lana was lost in thoughts and questions that her consciousness kept demanding answers to, ‘Why does really he like me?’, ‘Why did he suddenly invite me to a date?’, ‘Should I even go? Should I trust him? He is a stranger after all’. Her mother noticed that Lana was unusually quiet as if in deep thought, “Lana honey, how are you doing? Something troubling you?” Lana snapped out of her world, “Oh, hi mom, I am fine, just enjoying peace.” She chuckled, trying to change the topic but her mom caught the slight uncertainty in Lana’s tone.

“See honey, I have told this many times, no need to hide it from me.” Lana’s heart softened at her mother’s understanding of her emotions, “Look mom, there is this guy...” she began softly, “At the diner who I serve almost everyday.” Her mother was intrigued, “He once gave me his number and when I confronted him about it, he told me he likes me and asked me to come on a date with him at the community park.”

Lana’s mother had a shocked expression at first but she was delighted and smiled, “Oh sweetie, seems like you got someone too.” She teased before getting serious, “If you like him too, then sure, go ahead and attend the date.” Lana nodded, “I... kinda do like him a bit, mom.” She confessed shyly as her mother laughed and hugged her, “I know you, my sweet child. Just stay careful, okay?”. Her mother smiled maternally, holding Lana close.

New day, new life. Lana got dressed, applied some perfume which he father had bought an year ago and went to the community park to meet Adrian. As expected, the mysterious man was sitting there already, waiting for Lana to arrive. Upon sighting Lana, Adrian smoothed out his shirt and sat straighter on the bench before forming a charming smile on his face. “Greetings, nice to meet you.” Adrian greeted before standing up and shaking hands with Lana. “Nice to meet you too.” Lana shot a brief smile before sitting down on the bench.

“I didn’t quite expect you to agree so easily to my invitation.” Adrian initiated the conversion smoothly, “Though I am beyond delighted that you made it in time.” Lana felt a soft flutter in her chest, “Thank you, I was actually consulting my mother about this and I told her everything to gauge her reaction.” Adrian’s face had an enigmatic smile as he listened to what Lana did—almost as if she made something easier for him without knowing.

“Oh wow, I didn’t expect that to be honest, your mother knows me too now.”—“Yeah she does, but not personally.” Lana replied with a small smile, “So, tell me more about yourself and why you chose to date me out of all the other girls in the Diner?.” Adrian thought for a moment, selecting his words before answering.

“Well, I am Adrian murphy, a extroverted person who loves to drink coffee, I guess.” He chuckled, not revealing too much information about himself yet. “Oh... Adrian murphy, nice name, I am Lana rose, a simple, kind—hearted girl who likes nature and to decorate tables.”

Adrian listened attentively, “Great! So that explains why you work at the Diner as a waitress, hmm?” he mumbled teasingly in such a way that Lana couldn’t hear half of his sentence. “Excuse me, what were you saying?” Lana asked curiously, “Oh nothing, I was saying that its great knowing we both have unique qualities.” He recovered, thankful that Lana didn’t hear his teasing remark.

Soon, evening arrived as Lana and Adrian chatted along without realizing how much time they had spent. “Oh I guess it has gotten a bit late, isn’t it?” Adrian said, glancing around the now almost empty park since people left. “Oh yeah, uhm, if you don’t mind, my mother asked me to return back before evening because she is concerned about my safety especially since I am alone and out of home.”

Suddenly, Lana’s eyes landed on a man standing not so far from them, as she focused her sight, the man oddly resembled her father but just as she blinked, the man was gone. She gripped Adrian’s arm by instinct, trying to calm nerves. Adrian noticed this and grew concerned, “Hey, is everything okay? You seem stressed all of a sudden.” Lana snapped back to reality and realized her hand was gripping Adrian’s arm, she immediately pulled away, her cheeks turning pink. “Oh, I am really sorry, it was just, never mind, I am sometimes clumsy.” Adrian then offered something which made Lana breathless, “Let me escort you back home.” Lana hesitated for a brief moment, she had never been escorted home by someone but Adrian had this safe aura about him which unexplainably put her at some level of ease. “S-sure I guess..” Lana agreed and they both began walking back home.

While walking, Lana managed to probe, “Hey, why are you being so kind to me, offering me things without me really asking for it, considering we both are still technically strangers.” Adrian chuckled, “I think you may have forgot that I am a kind person, and talking about being strangers, I think we both have gained some familiarity and understanding of each other, haven’t we?”

“Oh really? You think one date was enough?” Lana teased back—for the first time ever that she had spoken this confidently with another man. “So you’re asking we go on more dates?”—“Not really asking, but wouldn’t mind.” Soon, they reached home and after saying goodbye to each other, they both went to their homes.

As the front door was shut behind Lana, her mother smiled and came to greet her, “Sweetie, how was your date today?” Lana smiled back, hugging her mother. “It was amazing, mom, we had a lot of good talk and he is a really nice, really kind guy. He also escorted me back home.” Her mother seemed surprised by the act of kindness that Adrian showed, “Really? That’s so thoughtful of that man, he really seems good even though I haven’t met him.” Lana nodded in agreement.

“He seems like the perfect match for you though.” Her mother nudged, causing Lana to blush hard and hide her face, “Oh my goodness mom, stop it.” She stifled a shy giggle as her mother smiled in amusement, “What sweetie? That’s how your father and I got married.” Her mother had a nostalgic expression while at the mention of her father, Lana remembered seeing that one man who very closely resembled her father and that brought a fresh wave of anxiety to her.

“Well, I made dinner for you, honey. Get fresh and come eat your meal.” Her mother instructed and went to the kitchen as Lana changed into a comfortable pyjama set and ate her meal before going to bed. While scrolling through social media, she received a message from Adrian....

Chapter 3

As she received the message, her heart raced with excitement, fingers flying over the screen as she opened the message and read it, “Hey Lana, Adrian here. After our date, I can’t stop thinking about you, it just feels like you’re the one for me.” This proved to be simply too much for Lana to comprehend at that moment as she practically jumped up from her bed, eyes wide in surprise as she read the same text over and over again just to confirm what she was reading.

Her fingers trembled in nervousness and excitement as she typed back, “W-what? Adrian what are you saying? Are you serious?”—“Serious than ever.” The response came immediately as if Adrian was waiting for her text. Lana’s heart started thundering in her chest, she was struggling to process Adrian’s feelings as the big development happened so rapidly.

A bubble popped on her chat box, glowing softly as Adrian was typing and suddenly the text appeared, “What happened? Is everything ok?”. Adrian asked in concern, he felt that he may have pushed too far too quick that Lana might drift away from him but soon, his screen flashed with another message, “Yeah, everything is fine, just-just trying to make sense of your words.” Adrian sighed behind his screen, trying to be careful with her, “Look, sorry that I may have pushed too far too quick, I just, wanted to confess my feelings because I thought it would be better to do rather than holding them in my heart.”

Lana’s eyes scanned each word, heart hammering against her very own ribcage as if fighting to burst out of her chest, adrenaline coursing through her veins. ‘Is this real, does Adrian really like me that much? But we just met and only had one date. How does he have such deep feelings for me already?’ the question rang in her brain, demanding an answer which Lana was hesitant to coax out of Adrian.

Slowly, she took a breath, calmed herself before replying to his message, her whole body practically on fire, “Can I ask why you harbour such strong feelings for me? We’d only had one date and it had such a great effect on you? I had no idea.” She hit send and the message indicator turned blue, signalling that Adrian had read the message.

“You see, I wasn’t just observing since the date, I noticed you since you started serving me at the Diner, the way you carry yourself with grace caught my eye, and my heart.” As the text appeared on Lana’s screen, her cheeks embraced a soft pink tint from shyness, she had never expected Adrian to be this expressive, this confident in what he spoke.

“Your words don’t sound like just words, they sound meaningful.” Adrian’s lips curved upward into a smile at Lana’s praise. “Thanks I guess?” He typed back as Lana laughed behind her screen, a genuine laugh emerging from her after a long time, she had even forgotten when she last laughed like this—not pretending to, but actually laughing.

“You’re very sappy, you know that?” Lana grinned as she hit send and waited for Adrian’s text, “Sappy for you.” The response came, causing Lana to gasp in amusement and cover her face to stifle a laugh forming in her throat, ‘Oh god he is trying to be charming.’ Lana thought to herself, barely managing to not burst into heavy laughter. She eventually composed herself, her eyes glinting with mischief, “So, what do you plan to do next?”.

“Lets meet again, what do you say?”, Adrian typed back and Lana’s eyes perked up in excitement, “Oh wow, a second date? Sounds good to me. Which day are you planning?”.

Adrian thought for a moment, before disclosing the time, “How about tomorrow?” Lana felt a rush of anticipation crawl through her nerves, her hands trembling a bit in excitement, “Sure thing! How about 4 p.m. tomorrow?”

The next thing they knew, they were meeting at 4 p.m. the other day. Adrian waited patiently for Lana who was a bit late once again but didn’t mind it, Lana came and sat opposite to Adrian across the table, “Sorry for being a bit late, though I must say, you have a nice taste in cafes.” Lana commented and Adrian giggled, leaning forward a bit with the menu in his hand, “I already ordered the food too.” Lana’s eyes widen in surprise, “oh? Quick service?” she playfully said and Adrian nodded, watching as their food was being served moments later before them.

Lana gasped in shock, her stomach gurgling in hunger at the sight of her favourite food being served in front of her. She took a moments longer glance at the food and then back at Adrian who had a smile on his face as if saying “surprise”. Lana was speechless for a few minutes before smiling back, genuinely, she reached across the table and held Adrian’s hand in hers, “How did you know about my favourite food?” she inquired, studying Adrian’s face intently, “Oh you forgot? You told me when we were at our first date.” Realization dawned upon Lana and she found herself silly.

“Oh yeah, I just have the memory of a goldfish.” She laughed in a bit of a self-depreciation but Adrian reassured her, “No, it happens to most of us, even me, its okay to forget about things sometimes, don’t judge yourself for it.” He gave Lana’s hand a gentle squeeze, making her smile grow brighter.

“Thank you Adrian, you’re the person who truly sees me sometimes, truly accepts me, flaws and all.” Adrian smiled back, “Oh you’re making me shy like this.” After a brief silence, Adrian gestured to their food, “Shall we get started?” Lana gained back her composure, “Yeah, right, sorry.” She giggled, diving into her food.

“mmm, this is delicious!” She exclaimed, taking bite after bite but suddenly, one bite came from Adrian, his hand extended across the table and the bite hovering inches from Lana’s face. Lana reluctantly took the bite, “Thanks”, she mumbled while chewing, a small smile on her face as the gesture made her feel special. Later, they went to take a stroll at the beach side at night. The moon shone bright on the surface, water lapping at the shore in a dance, the sky and sand illuminated in a blueish hue. The scent of fresh seawater filled the air. The gentle breeze caressed their skins, making them tingle.

Lana inhaled softly, clearing out her lungs and enjoying the breath of fresh air. “This is so amazing, the weather is absolutely gorgeous.”—“I know right? But I have something which is even more amazing.” With that, he knelt down before her, put his hand into his pocket and pulled out a black box, presenting it before Lana. As he opened the box, revealing a cute golden ring adorned with a heart shaped diamond sitting in the middle of the structure of the ring. Lana’s heart nearly stopped beating at the sight, everything forgotten in that moment except the beautiful ring in front of her eyes.

She stayed perfectly still like a statue, frozen in pure adoration and shock of the situation which unfolded so swiftly for her mind to process it properly, “W-what?” she slowly reached out with a trembling finger, trying to feel the ring beneath her very fingertips but Adrian took the ring from the box, gently grabbed Lana’s finger and slowly slid the ring onto it.

Lana gasped as the cool metal made contact with her skin, she kept gazing at the magnificent piece of jewellery on her hand as if it was the most precious thing on Earth. Her brain was so occupied with it that she didn’t even realize the implication of Adrian’s action. “Wow-... just wow..” Were the only words which escaped Lana’s lips. “You like it?” upon Adrian’s question, Lana looked at him immediately, her expression unreadable for a moment, “like? That’s an understatement, I LOVE it!” Lana squealed, throwing her arms around him in a tight hug, “Why did you do this? Why did you spend so much?”

Adrian simply held her before replying, “I was just feeling romantic.” Lana laughed, tears of joy escaping her eyes as a wide smile spread across her face, “Such a nice, romantic proposal, I accept.” Lana declared, completely happy and satisfied by her choice to spend the rest of her life with the man she felt safe and loved the most. “I promise to keep you happy, support you in your toughest times, provide for you and make you feel safe, respected and heard.” Lana grew emotional at the sincerity in Adrian’s voice, “Oh Adrian, you already do all of that without me even asking, you’re the best.” She declared further, hugging him back.

“So, wedding when?” Adrian asked teasingly, trying to lighten the emotional moment and Lana managed to grin through her tears, “When do you say, darling?” Adrian playfully raised an eyebrow at Lana’s usage of words, “Darling, huh? Well, how about we keep the wedding some weeks later, till then we decide the hall, prepare clothes, invite friends and stuff, I am thinking of keeping it decent and modest.” Lana nodded, “Perfect thinking, my future husband-to-be.” She said with a slight giggle, leaning in to kiss Adrian and he reciprocated, both couples enjoyed their time under the moon.

Chapter 4

With their plans finalized for the wedding, Lana and Adrian now had to break it to their parents. Lana went home after their romantic date and found her mother sitting on the couch, eating dry fruits. Her mother’s eyes perked up at the sight of her daughter and she hugged Lana tightly, “Lana sweetie, where were you?” she asked, her voice tinged with maternal concern. “I was with Adrian, mom.” Lana replied softly, relishing the feeling of her mother’s warm and comforting embrace.

“Oh, out with that gentleman again without telling me?” her mother teased playfully, patting Lana’s back who chuckled, “ah, sorry mom, I must have forgotten to inform you prior, I apologize.” Her mother’s expression turned a bit serious but it didn’t lose its maternal love and concern, “Just remember to inform me every time you go out, okay baby?” she gave Lana a gentle kiss on the forehead.

“Yes mom, I promise to keep you informed about my plans, and talking about plans, I have something to tell you...” Her cheeks embraced a soft, pinkish tone which gave away her shyness. Lana’s mother raised an eyebrow, wondering what her daughter could possibly have to tell, “Speak up, darling, what is it?” she was fully invested into what Lana was about to spill. “I, I uhm, have some really interesting plans regarding Adrian. I have discussed those with him too.”

Lana’s mother was now fully curious and intrigued, “Come on darling, what is it that you want to tell mommy about? What plans do you have?” Lana inhaled, preparing to reveal the inevitable, her chest rising and falling in anticipation, “Well mom, I- I plan to.. uhm, I ...” she stuttered, trying to gain the confidence, “I plan on... marrying him.” As soon as the words left her lips, she felt an odd sensation in her chest as if she had done something bad, though it was part excitement and part nervousness. Her mother stayed silent for a few moments, deeply contemplating the decision of her daughter, she weighed the risks and rewards equally, knowing well that without the guidance of Lana’s father, it wouldn’t be easy for her to oversee such a big responsibility but it wasn’t impossible.

“What happened, mom?” Lana tentatively asked, worried that her mother may not allow the marriage in protectiveness for her daughter but her mother let go off the stress and focused back on Lana, “Yes sweetie, everything is okay, I was just lost in thought, sorry for that.” She held Lana close once again, stroking her hair, “You see honey, without your father being here, this all will become a bit challenging for me to arrange but I am willing to take the step if you’re fully happy and satisfied with it.”

Upon hearing her mother’s decision, Lana beamed with joy, fresh tears exited her eyes as she hugged her mother even tighter, still struggling to fully express her gratitude “Thank you so much, mom, I can’t explain to you on how happy I am that you’ve allowed me to marry him.” She said through her happy tears. Lana’s mother had her own tears streaming down her cheeks as she envisioned a future of her daughter with an another man. “I also miss dad a lot, he was like a beacon of support and love for me.” Hearing this from Lana brought fresh tears to her mother’s eyes as she went through the memories of her husband.

“I know, my sweet child, I know, your dad was a great person, a great father and great husband, I still see his face while doing daily chores in the house. Feels like he smiles at us from the heavens.” Lana nodded, “Me too, mom, me too, one time when I was at the park with Adrian, I saw a guy who looked just like dad, but oddly enough, the guy seemed to have disappeared from broad sight.” Lana’s mother seemed confused at this, “What do you mean, dear?”—“I don’t know, mom, maybe it was an hallucination, god knows.”

“It is possible that it was your own imagination and that you were just thinking a lot about dad.” Her mother explained gently and Lana confessed, “I was, I was actually thinking about dad while walking towards the park to meet Adrian.” She continued, “I kept thinking about those moments when dad used to take me to the park when I young, the fun we used to have and all..” her voice took on a dreamy quality as she relived the memories of her time with her father.

Her mother listened intently, smiling in wonder as her daughter recalled the memories so passionately. Lana turned her head to face her mother, smiling, “Thank you mom, for being there for me since day one, for being supportive of me and encouraging me in every step, every aspect of life.” She hugged her mother, conveying her affection and love. “Of course my sweet, you’re my daughter and its my duty to provide for you as your mother.”

After spending some time together, Her mother remembered she had to cook and got up quickly, “Alright honey, enough cuddles, let me make dinner now.” Her mother said before heading to the Kitchen, Lana went to her room to change. After getting permission from her mother regarding the marriage, Lana wasted no time in informing Adrian as soon as possible. She texted him back with barely contained excitement, her fingers trembling due to the adrenaline of the moment. “Oh my god Addy, you won’t believe it!!” she vigorously hit send, her energy evident.

“What? Believe what?” the response came back as quick as the message was sent, causing Lana’s eyes to light up even further. “MOM AGREED FOR OUR MARRIAGE!” She typed out in all capitals as a way of showing her joy to Adrian who responded back in kind “Really!? No way! I thought she’d probably say to wait or something.”

“No silly, my mom is the best and most amazing of moms, she agrees with me on almost anything, except if its dangerous for me.” she replied, giggling behind the screen as a text message popped up from Adrian “oh really? That’s great to hear, tells me your mom is a supportive one. Appreciate that!” Adrian’s comment made Lana smile genuinely, “Thank you Addy, but now tell me, have your parents agreed to the marriage?”

Upon seeing her question, Adrian chuckled and typed, “Well yes, though my parents said they wanted to see you in person to know you better before allowing me to marry you.” Lana burst out laughing as she had expected that answer, “Oh of course, our parents always want to see who we marry, its like a security feature thingy, well, my mom would also like to see you too of course.”

Adrian was surprised as he thought he wouldn’t have to meet Lana’s mom since he was shy, “Oh kidding? Really?” Lana laughed again before confirming. “Well, I can talk with your mom, just gotta deal with your dad since dads are more picky for their daughters, you know.” Adrian hit Lana with a stereotype joke, unaware of the effect his joke may have had on Lana.

Behind the screen, Lana saw the message, her eyes scanning every word and when she saw the mention of her father, it felt like someone hit her with a ton of bricks, her grip on her phone tightening hard as the room seemed to fade away with only her screen visible to her, the air felt dense. Her mind realizing the absence of her father once again, she didn’t feel angry at Adrian’s joke, she knew he was unaware of the event that had occurred but she felt hurt since her father had passed away a long time ago.

A single tear rolled down her cheek as she imagined her father still alive, probably beaming with joy upon hearing that Lana was finally marrying someone, unfortunately for Lana, her father departed this world before he could witness his daughter being married to a man she loved, to someone who would take care of her for the rest of her life.

She finally built back the courage to inform Adrian about the thing she had kept hidden for a reason even she didn’t knew. “Uhm, actually, I have no father.” she typed back with shaky fingers, causing Adrian to instantly feel guilty for making the joke as he got the information. “Oh, I... I am actually really sorry Lana. That’s really sad to hear. Im so sorry for your loss.. I shouldn’t have made a joke like that then.”

“Its ok.” Lana responded, simple, clean and straight to the point. “I feel bad that you don’t have a father anymore, may he have the highest place in heaven though.” Lana felt a sense of relief and gratitude as Adrian apologised and wished her deceased father well. A smile forming through her soft tears, “Thanks Adrian, you’re more mature than any other man I have seen.” The compliment in exchange made Adrian smile back even if they both stared at their screens.

“So, when should we meet each other’s parents?” Adrian questioned, wanting to be careful with Lana after learning that she has no father. “Hmm, maybe tomorrow you can visit my place and day after tomorrow I visit yours?” Lana suggested and Adrian seemed intrigued, “Okay, sounds like a plan. I’ll be at your place at 4 tomorrow.” Lana’s excitement came back as she read the message, already planning about tomorrow. “Perfect, I’ll make sure to cook something amazing for you.” Lana typed back, smiling, setting her phone down.

Chapter 5

The next day, Adrian arrived at Lana’s place at exactly the time he had promised yesterday on text. Lana squealed in delight as she opened the door and hugged Adrian “Im so happy and excited that you’re here, come on in.” she chirped, eyes sparkling with mischief. Adrian chuckled, walking into her house and noticing how modest and down to earth the interior seemed. Upon entering the living room, Adrian’s gaze landed on Lana’s mother and he immediately set his posture before greeting her mother to put a good first impression of himself.

“Greetings, Aunt.” Adrian said respectfully, standing before Lana’s mother as she smiled maternally, nodding at Adrian’s greeting to signal that she acknowledged it, “Greetings to you too, my child, come have a seat.” She gestured for Adrian to sit on the couch opposite to her and Adrian complied. Lana disappeared in the kitchen, only to appear moments later holding a tray containing fruit juice and biscuits. She elegantly placed the edible items in front of Adrian before sitting besides him, facing her mother.

The conversation began with Lana introducing Adrian, “So mom, as I told you, this is Adrian, the man whom I plan to marry.” Adrian felt a blush creeping up his cheeks at being assessed by Lana’s mother. “Oh, a nice gentleman you are, dear, tell me more about yourself, would you?” Lana’s mother inquired, seeking to gain more knowledge of the man who won her daughter’s heart. Adrian cleared his throat, composing himself before answering the asked question.

“So Aunt, I work as an engineer at a company in the city centre and I come from a middle class background. Apart from that, I and Lana felt something common in between us and we went on a few dates to see if the sparks between us were real which they were and slowly we uhm, grew fond of each other.” Lana’s mother nodded, listening intently.” Also, I can provide Lana with a house and anything she wants as I am financially capable of taking care of our needs and along with that, I would make Lana feel respected, heard for and cared in a family.”

Lana felt a rush of respect and admiration for Adrian. Her cheeks flushing with shyness due to the bold promises Adrian made. Her mother listened respectfully, before thinking about it herself,” A young man who is an engineer and has already planned for his marital future.” Lana’s mother assessed with critical eye, explaining the dynamic to Adrian.

“You see dear, ever since her father passed away, its only me and her who run this house and ever since he left us, I’ve grown more careful and protective of my daughter, since its a mother’s duty to provide and protect her children and considering the aspect of marrying my daughter. I have to make sure as a responsible mother that my daughter goes into the right hands and that she has a prosperous future ahead of her.”

Several months later. The Murphy household feels completely different. The silence is no longer oppressive. The nightmares have stopped. Lana laughs again. She decorates the dining table exactly like she used to at the diner. Adrian returns home from work one evening. He finds Lana baking cookies with Samantha and Mary while Charles pretends to steal freshly baked ones. For the first time...

The house feels alive. Later that evening Adrian and Lana walk through the same community park where they had their very first date. They sit on the same bench. The sunset paints the lake orange. Lana quietly leans her head against Adrian’s shoulder. Neither of them speaks for several minutes. She simply watches families walking by, children laughing and birds flying overhead. Eventually she breaks the silence. “When all of this started... I thought I was losing myself.” Adrian gently intertwines his fingers with hers. “I know. I couldn’t tell what was real anymore.” She lets out a small laugh. “Sometimes I even questioned whether you were real.” Adrian smiles softly. “I’m still here. You never left.” Lana looks up at him, her eyes carrying none of the fear they once held. Only warmth. Only peace. She places a hand against his cheek. “You know...When I first met you at the diner, I thought you were simply another customer.”

She laughs quietly. “I never imagined you’d become the person who kept me standing.” Adrian chuckles. “I just loved you.” Lana shook her head, correcting him, “You did much more than that.” She squeezes his hand. “When my own mind betrayed me...When fear blurred everything around me... When I couldn’t distinguish memories from nightmares...You stayed.” She pauses. A gentle breeze rustles the trees around them. “You reminded me who I really was.” Adrian brushes a loose strand of hair behind her ear. “I only did what any husband should.” Lana slowly shakes her head. “No.” She smiles through misty eyes.

“You became something far greater.” Her voice softens into barely more than a whisper. “You are my tether to reality.” Adrian’s expression melts into quiet emotion. He pulls her into a gentle embrace. “I’ll always be.” The two remain seated together as the last rays of sunlight disappear beyond the horizon. Around them, the park feels exactly as it had years ago when two strangers first met beneath its trees. Only now, they are no longer strangers trying to understand each other. They are husband and wife who had endured grief, fear, guilt and uncertainty together, emerging stronger because neither had let go of the other’s hand. For the first time in many years, Lana looked toward the distance and saw nothing waiting in the shadows. Only the gentle breeze dancing through the trees. Only the quiet certainty that she was finally home.

As time passed, their relationship grew stronger, more joyful and Lana was deeply grateful to have found a husband like Adrian. Over the course of the next few years, both had one daughter and were happy with her. Lana happily named her Maria. “My sweet daughter will grow up to be strong like mommy, right?” little Maria nodded, snuggling into Lana cutely. “Yes mom, I’ll be your strong daughter.” Lana smiled maternally, holding the little bundle of joy close to her. Adrian smiled, happy to have such a caring wife like Lana and an amazing daughter like Maria. As time passed, Maria grew up slowly but surely. Completing her education and graduating from a prestigious university, making her parents extremely proud. Finally Lana and Adrian wanted to get Maria married so she could have a partner who would support her and protect her. Unfortunately, Adrian got diagnosed with stage-4 cancer and his survival chances were slim, though he reassured that he would recover, and if he didn’t, he instructed Lana to marry Maria to a good man...

The End
